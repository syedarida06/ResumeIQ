About ResumeIQ

ResumeIQ is a web-based resume analysis platform that helps students, fresh graduates, and job seekers evaluate their resumes through skill detection, profile analysis, and personalized insights. The platform is designed with a clean, interactive interface to make resume evaluation simple and effective.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help users create stronger resumes by identifying skill gaps, providing meaningful insights, and encouraging continuous improvement throughout their career journey.

Our Vision

We envision ResumeIQ as a platform that supports career readiness by making resume analysis accessible, easy to understand, and useful for every learner and professional. We aim to promote career readiness by making resume improvement accessible, user-friendly, and valuable for learners, job seekers, and professionals across different career stages.

Who Can Use ResumeIQ?

🎓 Students

Build placement-ready resumes for internships and campus recruitment.

💼 Job Seekers

Improve existing resumes before applying for new career opportunities.

🚀 Fresh Graduates

Create professional resumes that showcase skills, projects, and achievements.
